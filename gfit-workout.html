FITNESS GUIDE

30 Minute Home Workout

Boost your vitality with precision routines designed to burn calories and strengthen core muscles without the need for expensive equipment. Efficient, clinical, and measurable.

What do you need?

2x2 Meter Space

Enough space to move freely without obstruction.

Drinking Bottles

Staying hydrated is key to peak performance during training.

Yoga Mat

Optional, for joint comfort when performing floor exercises.

Exercise Sequence

Follow these 5 basic movements for optimal results. Perform each movement with complete control.

Total Duration: 30 Minutes
1
WARMUP

Jumping Jacks

Start with light cardio to increase your heart rate and blood circulation throughout your body. Make sure your hands are touching above your head.

3 Minutes
Low
2
LEG STRENGTH

Bodyweight Squats

Strengthen your thigh and glute muscles. Keep your back straight and make sure your knees don't go past your toes as you lower.

3 Sets x 15
Medium
3
UPPER BODY

Standard Push-Ups

Focus on your chest, shoulders, and triceps. If this is too difficult, you can place your knees on the floor as a modification.

3 Sets x 12
High
4
CORE MUSCLES

Forearm Plank

Hold this position to build core stability. Be careful not to drop or raise your hips too high.

1 Minute
Medium
5
COOLING DOWN

Full Body Stretch

Finish with static stretching to aid muscle recovery and prevent injury after an intense workout.

5 Minutes
Low

Pro Tips: Performance & Precision

Get maximum results by paying attention to the small but crucial details in every movement.

1.) Breathe Correctly: Exhale during the hardest part of the movement (exertion).

2.) Quality over Quantity: It's better to do 5 perfect squats than 15 messy ones.

3.) Mind-Muscle Focus: Feel the muscles working for better muscle fiber activation.
