Balanced Meal Planning

Improve your health and support your fitness goals with a balanced nutrition plan designed to provide sustained energy, support overall wellness, and maintain long-term metabolic health.

How to Build Your Portion

Use the portion method to ensure you get all the macronutrients and micronutrients your body needs every day.

🍗

Protein

Fill ¼ of your plate with high-quality protein sources such as chicken, fish, eggs, tofu, or tempeh to support muscle growth and repair.

Nutrition Essentials
🌾

Carbohydrates

Choose complex carbohydrates such as brown rice, oats, sweet potatoes, or whole grains to provide long-lasting energy and maintain stable blood sugar levels.

Energy Stability
🥑

Healthy Fats

Include healthy fats from sources such as avocados, nuts, seeds, and olive oil to support hormone production and overall health.

Hormonal Health
🥦

Vegetables

Fill ½ of your plate with a variety of colorful vegetables to provide essential vitamins, minerals, and dietary fiber.

Fiber & Micronutrients
